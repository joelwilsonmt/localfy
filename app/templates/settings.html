Settings

Downloads

Set via the DOWNLOAD_PATH environment variable.
YouTube audio is ~128 kbps AAC or 160 kbps Opus — that's the quality ceiling regardless of format. opus and m4a are the best choices: ffmpeg can remux the original stream without re-encoding, avoiding the generation loss of transcoding to mp3. mp3 has the widest device compatibility. Lossless formats (flac, wav) produce much larger files with no quality benefit.
auto keeps the original YouTube stream's bitrate (no transcode — best quality/speed). A fixed bitrate re-encodes to that target; since the source is already ~128–160 kbps, higher values just grow the file without adding quality.

Scanning library…

    Your music folder is on a filesystem without hardlinks (exFAT and FAT don't have them), so these copies can't be merged here. Nothing is wrong with the files; they just take the extra space.

    The same track downloaded into several playlist folders is stored once per folder. Deduplicating replaces the extra copies with hardlinks — every folder keeps its file, but the audio is stored on disk only once. Needs a filesystem with hardlinks (most Linux and macOS ones; not exFAT or FAT), with all playlist folders on the same one.
